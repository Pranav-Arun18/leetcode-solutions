## Problem: Two Sum (Easy)

**Link:** LeetCode Two Sum

### Approach

The program checks the array elements to find two numbers whose sum
is equal to the given target. When the required pair is found, their
indices are returned.

### Complexity

- Time: O(n log n)
- Space: O(1)

### Notes

The solution should handle cases where the required two numbers are
not adjacent in the array.
